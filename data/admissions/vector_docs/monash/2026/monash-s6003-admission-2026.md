# Master of Mathematics 2026 录取与学制要求

以下内容由 CampusPilot 从学校官方 Handbook 的 Minimum entry requirements 提取。学制是标准路径；个人获批学分减免后，实际就读时间可能更短。

## 2年制项目
- 内部路径代码: ENTRY_LEVEL_1
- 需完成学分: 96
- 最低均分线: 65.0
- 相关专业背景要求: 未明确要求
- 相关工作经验: 未明确要求

96 points to complete – Duration: 2 years full-time, 4 years part-time

An Australian bachelor degree (or equivalent) with at least high credit (65%) average or equivalent qualification and experience approved by the faculty; and a 65% average over 4 units in mathematics of which at least 2 are at level 2.
## 1.5年制项目
- 内部路径代码: ENTRY_LEVEL_2
- 需完成学分: 72
- 最低均分线: 65.0
- 相关专业背景要求: 未明确要求
- 相关工作经验: 未明确要求

72 points to complete – Duration: 1.5 years full-time, 3 years part-time

An Australian bachelor degree (or equivalent) in mathematics with at least high credit (65%) average or equivalent qualification and experience approved by the faculty.

OR

An Australian graduate certificate/diploma (or equivalent) with at least high credit (65%) average or equivalent qualification and experience approved by the faculty; and a 65% average over 8 units in mathematics.
## 1年制项目
- 内部路径代码: ENTRY_LEVEL_3
- 需完成学分: 48
- 最低均分线: 65.0
- 相关专业背景要求: 未明确要求
- 相关工作经验: 未明确要求

48 points to complete – Duration: 1 year full-time, 2 years part-time

A four-year Australian honours degree (or equivalent) in mathematics with at least high credit (65%) average or equivalent qualification and experience approved by the faculty.
